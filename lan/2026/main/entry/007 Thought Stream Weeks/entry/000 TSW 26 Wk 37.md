## A New thought stream!

2026-09-07 Wk 37 Mon - 21:04 +03:00

Yay! We have a *public* Weekly thought stream now!

It was inspired by `~satori` here: https://tilde.club/~satori/

I like the banner!

But the inspiration is about their `TalkBox`:

 > 
 > I like to ramble but not in a way long enough or structured enough to make blog posts so…welcome to the talkbox! Home of “stream of consciousness” style rambles!

There are many ways to call it, but I feel similarly. This is why I have Thought Streams to ease the writing process and capture things in the moment.

I've had those longer in a private note repo, but I've had some here, like

* [001 Gentoo Usage Thought Stream](../../004%20Configuring%20my%20gentoo%20system/entry/001%20Gentoo%20Usage%20Thought%20Stream.md)

Those are more topic-based, so I'll signal I'm jumping into em here, but a global talkbox or thought stream is also nice.

Stuff to try!

In [001 Write small posts with a clear goal and audience as inspired by Julia Evans](../../../../post/001%20Write%20small%20posts%20with%20a%20clear%20goal%20and%20audience%20as%20inspired%20by%20Julia%20Evans.md), I wrote

I think the same thing applies, if a post feels "too heavy", and you want to leave a passing kind remark or an observation, we can also interact through something like a talkbox/thought stream!

A dev log like Satori has could be cool too. Like a space to record key happenings and progress. Though I'm calling it an [act log](../../006%20Now%20Logs/entry/001%20Weekly%20Act%20Log.md) since I'll intend for it to be less changelog-y and more a place for possible commentary on what I've done or demos maybe.

Satori also links to other websites with really cool design like https://melankorin.net/! And there, melankorin has a cool [gallery](https://melankorin.net/gallery/) and [art log](https://melankorin.net/gallery/log/)!

There are many other websites at https://tilde.club/, where apparently get access to a unix system shell to put their websites! You'd be surprised what you can find.

Not sure if I can be on there yet since I've yet to create a cool website for my stuff, besides serving them directly off of codeberg! It's really all just markdown. I do a one pass filtering so that they appear well in there in a `webview` branch though. But there are disagreements between github and codeberg on how that is interpreted.

Note that my thought streams can actually vary in time. The last set of sentences are separated by a day. But when I come back and read the sentences, it is like a conceptual walk to a new thought and I add it incrementally to it.

## Blogging, warm heartfelt personal exploration, breezy cold investigations

2026-09-08 Wk 37 Tue - 08:17 +03:00

From https://melankorin.net/blog/2026/04/17/,

 > 
 > Isn’t the point of a blog to be *personal*? Am I just making people look at a perfect marble statue of me instead of… just me?

Thank you Kori for sharing your thoughts and experiences with dealing with the bads of perfectionism. I relate to being lost in your own little world, away from social life.  You also have a cool way you express yourself in dual voice, commenting on your own writing live!

I think the point of a blog or a personal website is whatever you want to make of it. It seems that how you relate to it is helping you strive for a more colorful and low-stakes space, while keeping in mind that you value quality in your work so much, so that is really cool.

For me also, this website is a place of play. And play can even be impersonal. I love to imagine myself as an investigator, surrounded by a world of clues, noticing where my thoughts go next, or what little detail is crucial for progress. But this is also personal in a way. The way I interact with this space and these practices also helps me explore myself, even when it may look cold breezy and impersonal, it is nonetheless personal in ways. A lot of the mental devices I play with are ways to deal with my own anxieties and also perfectionism. This thought stream is here because I just want to write without having to think I must provide value for someone. Perhaps it is of no value to anyone, and perhaps it is of bad quality, and- but look at it. It's here, flying in words and sentences and feelings. It was inspired by your writing. It will probably inspire me more to come, how I relate to my own writing, where I prefer the air to be cool, and where I prefer it to be warm.

In the end it's joyful and playful, and in many ways sorrowful and anxiety inducing what to choose to share here, or keep to my personal journals, or maybe even articulate to myself.

Thanks to `~satori` again for the Talkbox idea. This did not need to be long, or very deeply explored, but a space like this does not require me to justify that it needs to exist or not.

I also care about my work. I don't *want* it to be bad. But like I wrote in [019 Self judgment hinders learning by redirecting us away from technical signs to identity and narrative](../../../../topic/concept/000%20Atomic/wiki/003%20Wiki%20Clusterline%20Concepts/entry/019%20Self%20judgment%20hinders%20learning%20by%20redirecting%20us%20away%20from%20technical%20signs%20to%20identity%20and%20narrative.md), there is a grace even in failure. I do not want to make it seem as if failure is insignificant. It is significant especially because we respect our craft and want it to shine, but often the key lies in how we relate to the craft and how we act in it. Self-shame often distorts what failure could tell us about ourselves and the work.

We can explore the colorful, and colorless, and everything-in-between parts of our craft and ourselves in a more curious environment of play and investigation!

And we can try other things too, this frame also doesn't have to take the center of attention.
