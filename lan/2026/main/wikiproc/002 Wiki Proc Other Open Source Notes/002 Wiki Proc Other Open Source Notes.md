they give an interesting account of molecular notes (like atomic notes but captures a composed idea). although the example looks like it cleanly delegates subsections to atomic or other surface molecular notes. In zettlekasten style the links are also long bigints.

i think this reading of the molecular note is what i would consider an index note. it does make sense for molecular notes to delegate to other atomic notes but i think to some extent even molecular notes should explicate a singular structure that is more than the sum of its parts. this can happen when it doesnt simply list the parts but actively uses them to make arguments, synthesize ideas, or specialize the set of atomic notes to its particular context such that it can give a concrete particular account.

this is why i like andy matuschak's idea of full sentence sharp claim titles. these arent just to be pointed to, they can be actively used to compose essays and be a driving part for the synthesis of other sharp proposition notes.
