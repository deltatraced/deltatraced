# Journal

2026-09-01 Wk 36 Tue - 04:38 +03:00

It was in the context of a mystery game I played called the `The Incedient at Galley House` where I took private notes that I made serious use of markers and subcard threads.

Often the game presents you with incomplete information and clues but you *know* that you will eventually be able to tie the incomplete set of information to reveal the clue to progress the story.

I also made heavy use of holes in that mystery game, inspired by Agda's holes in the form of `?1, ?2, ` etc. For example when a character refers to another's sister, but I do not know who their sister is, I can identify them as `?1`.

Markers helped me be more resourceful with my effort, because I *can* do some partial effort but not to mystery completion. So the thread needs to be returned to. Hence it given a marker. Otherwise I would have to rely on memory, or redo effort to re-assemble evidence. It also helped in the game in pursuing multiple threads where I am making progress in more than one, if I am not making decisive progress but instead gaining partial information that helps here then there.

The game also made it clear how potent it is to be disciplined about citing your sources. The conversations the characters had was its own ground of truth, and regex references to specific character text in a given scene is then *interpreted* to make some inferences for the mystery. In other notes, we also should also be disciplined about this distinction. Notes should include references to the set of evidence that, when considered alongside the current context, should allow the investigator to draw a conclusion themselves.

[006 Signs of our practice](../../../wiki/003%20Wiki%20Clusterline%20Concepts/concept/006%20Signs%20of%20our%20practice.md) also writes on this distinction between the data sourced and the sign, which unifies it alongside an interpretation in a given context. The sign redirects the investigator towards the conclusion. Ideally, the context and the set of evidence makes this leap easy. But we can also explain and make an argument that synthesizes this evidence after listing it.
