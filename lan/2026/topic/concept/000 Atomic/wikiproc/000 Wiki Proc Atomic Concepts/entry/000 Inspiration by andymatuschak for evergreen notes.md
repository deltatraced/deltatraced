# Journal

2026-05-26 Wk 22 Tue - 00:28 +03:00

From this article, `Evergreen notes should be concept-oriented`: https://notes.andymatuschak.org/z2hQEhqWkdRLL9JUwfawZZx

They describe that notes should be conceptual rather than tied to events like projects and specific books read. The idea being that they would then be indexed by the project and not as their own atomic concept.

So I am making this wiki `Atomic Concepts` to host notes in a similar spirit. The idea is for minimal hierarchy, it is simply an index of concepts I write about and encounter. Top-level hierarchy is the fact that this is a project under a topic, or as one might call a domain: `Atomic Concepts`. The idea behind making it a topic is that we get one level of hierarchy extra. Every wiki cluster is now interpreted as a `subdomain`. The main domain indicates that we will follow the methodology of atomic concepts in all subdomains.

A possible subdomain is `Mathematics`. Problems (and associated proofs) will be of type `task`, and concepts will be of type `concept` for it.

Where we differ from the above article is that every wiki is associated with process notes. Those can still index by project, and journal changes to the wiki and why to keep the wiki as the latest record. This way conceptual *development* is not obscured. The development does take into account the individual projects and books, after all. But those details are kept away from the wiki, so that the wiki can act as a source of comparison and composition of ideas between sources.
