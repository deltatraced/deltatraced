In a typical [process](../concept/000%20Term%20Process%20Note.md) note cluster, we write our notes as they naturally arise, through spawn order and relations. However, this may not be the best representation. An automatic index similarly does not present the best way to look at the progression of a cluster. So we can create an `Overview X` note, where X is the cluster name, which provides a wiki document that guides the reader to the most relevant works and in a defined order.
