The clusterline format allows us to group both projects and clusters under a given status via `st/{status}`. One of those is the `mightdo`, which does not commit us to actually do it. Similar to [aspiring notes](../concept/001%20Term%20Aspiring%20Note.md), `mightdo` projects and clusters help us avoid clutter and mark a distinction between idea and intents we follow through.
