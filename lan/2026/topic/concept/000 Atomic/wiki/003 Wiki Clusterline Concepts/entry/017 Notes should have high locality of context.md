One factor in breaking down context is whether it is too fragmented where it doesn't make sense. We should strive to make atomic notes: [004 Atomicity of Context](../concept/004%20Atomicity%20of%20Context.md).

One point of tension here is that notes may get too fragmented. If we find ourselves jumping between many notes in a single session, we may be dealing with low locality of context. Just like cache memory, we should not have to jump around too much just to process some information. This does not mean that we do not make atomic notes and that we put everything in the same place, because then we will struggle to form strong connections between notes. However, we are able to synthesize new atomic notes from old ones that increase locality (ie. Makes you jump around less) and make use of those instead.
