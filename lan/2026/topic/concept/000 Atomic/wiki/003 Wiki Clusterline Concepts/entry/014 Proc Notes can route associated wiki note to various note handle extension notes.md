Because [007 Handles on note names can be used for modular extensions](007%20Handles%20on%20note%20names%20can%20be%20used%20for%20modular%20extensions.md), those various handles can be routed to a wiki note via its wikiproc associated note.

This helps hide the compositional complexity of process notes from the wiki notes which may be more reader-facing or concerned primarily with the document in question rather than various changes in it.
