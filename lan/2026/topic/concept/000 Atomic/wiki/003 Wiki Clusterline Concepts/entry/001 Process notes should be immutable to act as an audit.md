[Process notes](../concept/000%20Term%20Process%20Note.md) should be immutable to provide us with an audit of how their object (the outcome of the process documented) was created. If we alter history, then we lose audibility.

This immutable + log aspect of process notes makes them best thought of as append-only files for the most part, although it is possible to append at different parts of the file, so long as the timestamps are added out of order.
