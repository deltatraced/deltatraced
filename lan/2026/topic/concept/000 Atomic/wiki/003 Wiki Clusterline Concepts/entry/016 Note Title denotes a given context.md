How we title our notes is important. It should give us a very clear idea of the scope and responsibility of the note. Bad note titles are broad and in being broad they accumulate unrelated information and lower our ability to search for relevant information later on.

[006 Process notes are meant to be mined for evidence rather than linearly read](006%20Process%20notes%20are%20meant%20to%20be%20mined%20for%20evidence%20rather%20than%20linearly%20read.md) and this improves if notes do not deviate from their denoted scope. Their denoted scope can aid us in search, alongside content-wide search methods.
