Wiki clusters come in duals, every wiki entry may have associated process notes, or there may be process notes which do not map one to one to any given wiki entry. The wikiproc notes are differentiated with the note handle `Proc`.

To include `Proc` is to encode the expectation that there could be a dual wiki note, otherwise it is not necessary.
