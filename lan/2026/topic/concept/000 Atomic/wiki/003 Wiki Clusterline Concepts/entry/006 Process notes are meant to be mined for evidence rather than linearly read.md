A lot of writing has an audiance. It is written with a reader in mind, and refined to be easy to parse for its target audiance.

[Process notes](../concept/000%20Term%20Process%20Note.md), on the other hand, are a trail of encounters and key information. They are not meant to be easy to read, and they are not meant to just be browsed and read linearly. They divide context in an atomic fashion and present a minimum set of information for reconstruction.

Think of Process Notes as the reverse engineer's notes of reconstructing someone else's designs and intentions. They should be minimal, and high signal. Explanations for an audiance can take process notes into account to be written, in their own sections or other files (per [005 Process Notes may contain artifact sections in non journal headers](005%20Process%20Notes%20may%20contain%20artifact%20sections%20in%20non%20journal%20headers.md)).
