[Process Notes](../concept/000%20Term%20Process%20Note.md) can contain [artifact note](../concept/008%20Term%20Artifact%20Note.md) sections. These sections can be rewritten for clarity, or just removed, and give no promise of immutability (contra [001 Process notes should be immutable to act as an audit](001%20Process%20notes%20should%20be%20immutable%20to%20act%20as%20an%20audit.md)). These usually are produced from the Journal header, so that no information is lost.

One example is an issue has a `# Solution` header, and the `# Journal` writes the progression towards discovering the solution. This may then be copied over to the `# Solution` section and cleaned up, so long as we maintain that all the data needed to write that section is already in `# Journal`. This can be useful because it allows readers to quickly get the key value from a note, who would otherwise be uninterested in the historical aspects of the problem.
