Artifacts in general may be deliverables and documents expected to be refined to meet some objective. They are opposed to [000 Term Process Note](000%20Term%20Process%20Note.md), which act as audits and historical records instead.

Artifact notes are the default for wiki notes. They are the reason we have the wiki and wikiproc seperation, which is this change of the default expectation of a cluster.

Artifact notes can be freely revised, and a process note may track the knowledge work involved in justifying those revisions. They may be freely reader-facing or target specific audiance, wheras [006 Process notes are meant to be mined for evidence rather than linearly read](../entry/006%20Process%20notes%20are%20meant%20to%20be%20mined%20for%20evidence%20rather%20than%20linearly%20read.md)

This concept note is an artifact note, as it sits in a wiki cluster which by default hosts artifact notes.

Artifact notes may also be part of process notes, when placed in headers other than `# Journal`, as `# Journal` indicates the process note's chronological records.

Source code is also an artifact, although not a note, but they share similarities and are written as revisable documents usually intended to meet some set of expectations and be more easily shared with others and used.

Because of this, artifact notes can better utilize shared norms and formats, and may be made to synchronize with other note taking systems teams may use.
