Just as in the title of this project, `000 Atomic`, an atomic context or note is to be interpreted to mean that either

1. It is singular in purpose and does not mix multiple concerns. In a sense, it is basic (like a single task, a single question, a single reported issue, a single proposition, a single concept, etc.)
1. OR It is a singular composition of other basic notes. Some notes synthesize information of others, and so form a singular concern of synthesis of information.
   * Think of:
     * Overview notes which define how other notes should be navigated
     * Judgment notes which must decide on a policy by presenting an argument synthesizing evidence and reasons
     * Investigative question notes which ask a broad question but may decompose into more technical examinations
     * A note that synthesizes information from other notes for a single purpose.

Atomicity of context is balanced against locality of context: [017 Notes should have high locality of context](../entry/017%20Notes%20should%20have%20high%20locality%20of%20context.md)

Make use of [002 Serial Reduction of Context](002%20Serial%20Reduction%20of%20Context.md) to identify stages with high coupling of information to group them together, and [003 Parallel Reduction of Context](003%20Parallel%20Reduction%20of%20Context.md) to identify independent stages that could be worked on independently and have lower coupling to separate them and increase atomicity.
