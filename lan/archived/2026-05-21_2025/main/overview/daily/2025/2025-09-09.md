# 1 Day Overview

We created [deltachives](https://github.com/deltachives)! The purpose is to have a place for our experimental scripts and files and reproductions, but also to keep repositories focused on one purpose rather than creating a big unfocused repository. This can also help build a habit of creating many small libraries for many different problems which can then be reused in the future. More details are captured in the entry [Break down lan-exp-scripts into an org](https://github.com/LanHikari22/lan-setup-notes/blob/webview/lan/tasks/2025/000%20Note%20Repo%20Migration%20Sept%208/entries/000%20Break%20down%20lan-exp-scripts%20into%20an%20org.md)

A lot of the work is towards realizing [001 Sept 8 Obsidian note process change](../../../entry/001%20Sept%208%20Obsidian%20note%20process%20change.md). There are broadly three different directions to tackle

1. To resolve breaking changes for our time logger, this was fixed and now it recognizes child notes as subitems of parent notes as long as the property exists in the frontmatter in the child note: `parent: [[ my parent note]]`. Note that there are no grandchildren, a parent cannot have a parent.
1. An obsidian extension to turn the practice into single actions. There is a lot going on, we can spawn notes within a single big note folder, and then we have to refer to the exact line where the spawning occurred, and we need to link to the parent, and we need to update the frontmatter, and keep the Index in the big note up to date. Too many details and it's easy to mess it up. This should be done via actions.
1. Migration efforts. There are still many notes written in the old format. Huge files with many entries that should be broken into an island of many small files. One context per file.

Effort (1) is complete, (2) and (3) remain. Some work was also put in (3).

A weekly cumulative item summary of the effort can be found [here](https://github.com/LanHikari22/lan-setup-notes/blob/webview/scripts/templater/data/lan/weekly/Summary-2025-09-Wk37.md).

I explained some of my system ideas to a friend, here's a recap:

|Context Type|Meaning|
|------------|-------|
|Task|Operative. Has a clear end goal to reach, something to do. Akin to a tactic. Example: "Implement a prime number generator"|
|Goal|Deliverable. Describes what we want to exist before we set out to make it happen. It is a strategy where tasks are tactics.|
|Project|Projects orchastrate goals and phases to make the entire project mission succeed. May also include other administrative elements such as management of teams. Project $\to$ Goals $\to$ Tasks.|
|Issue|A Problem encountered. Focus on describing the state of affairs and make the problem reproducible, and then find a way to resolve it or change the state of affairs. Issues may spawn other issues.|
|HowTo|Knowledge checks. Whether consulting documentation, colleagues, google, or otherwise. There is a query, this describes the query and then captures the findings.|
|Investigation|Those includes conceptual inquiries, debugging sessions, etc. They are more open ended than a task in the sense that your question itself may need refinement. They can be asked in confusion and the investigation reveals a causal understanding of the subject matter. They reduce noise to signal!|
|Idea|Hypothetical things to do, brainstorming, capturing inspirations, etc.|
|Concept|Those have their own files, one per concept. But they are like the [σ-additive](../../../../../2026-05-21_2026/topic/concept/math/concepts/2025/018%20%CF%83-additive.md) concept. They can have definitions and associated examples, properties, theorems, etc. And they connect to other concepts to form a concept graph! Concepts do not refer to task items or similar. They may be referred to but they only refer to concepts, examples, demonstrations, or proofs, etc.|

My friend suggested adding a new context type `Inference` to the above table. Most things above are upstream, they're different ways of doing, but `Inference` would be the downstream effects and learnings. The ah-ha moments, or the realizations after the fact. So I am adding this with our new process change!
