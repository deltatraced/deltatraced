We start with defining the [natural numbers](../concepts/000%20Defn%20N.md). Already with the first two axioms, we can prove [3 is a natural number](../tasks/000%20three_nat%203%20is%20a%20natural%20number.md). But these two axioms are not enough to pin down the natural numbers, since they do not prevent wrap-around. We prove that we [4 is not 0](../tasks/001%20four_nz%204%20is%20not%200.md) to show that at least we cannot wrap back to 0.
