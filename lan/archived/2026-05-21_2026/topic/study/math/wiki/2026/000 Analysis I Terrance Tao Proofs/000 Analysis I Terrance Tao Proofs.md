I will record my proofs going through the book here. I have already started this effort by formalization in Rocq, but the idea here is to write human proofs. I take inspiration however from how one writes proofs in proof assistants like Agda and Rocq, so I will attempt to replicate some aspects in my [proof writing style](entries/001%20On%20Proof%20Writing%20Style.md) here.
